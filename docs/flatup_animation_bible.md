# FLATUP GYM Animation Bible v2.0

## Project Overview

**Official Name:** FLATUP GYM Animation Universe  
**Theme:** 世界一優しい格闘技ジム (The Gentlest Martial Arts Gym in the World)  
**Format:** Animated series (9:16 vertical, Pixar/Illumination quality)  
**Target:** Children 4-12 years old + families

---

## Core Concept

格闘技は「人を倒すため」ではなく「自分を育てるため」。  
子どもにも理解できるように、優しい世界観で表現する。

**Final Message:**  
強さとは人を傷つけることではなく、自分の心を育てることである。  
本当の強さとは、優しさである。

## Visual Style Guide

### Color Palette

| Element | Primary | Secondary |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| Sandbag | Neon Pink (#FF1493) | Bright Yellow (#FFD700) |
| Floor | Cream White (#FFFDD0) | - |
| Mats | Fresh Green (#90EE90) | - |
| Walls | Soft Beige (#F5F5DC) | - |
| Lighting | Warm Gold (Golden Hour) | - |
| Accent | Coral (#FF7F50) | Sky Blue (#87CEEB) |

### Animation Style

- **Reference:** Pixar + Illumination + Disney
- **Character Design:** 2.5〜3頭身 Chibi
- **Eyes:** Large, expressive, sparkling
- **Lighting:** Cinematic, volumetric, warm
- **Frame Rate:** 60fps target
- **Resolution:** 4K (2160p) → vertical 9:16

### Gym Environment

**Fixed Elements (to maintain consistency):**
- Green mats
- White floor
- Pink + Yellow sandbags (hanging)
- Wooden shelves + storage
- Potted plants (real vegetation)
- Large windows with natural light
- FLAT UP GYM sign (wooden, warm-lit)
- Mirrors
- Training equipment (mitts, gloves, headgear)

## Animation Rules

### Facial Expressions (7 Basic)

1. **Smile** - Joy, happiness, achievement
2. **Concentration** - Focus, effort, determination
3. **Surprise** - Wonder, discovery, amazement
4. **Sadness** - Disappointment, empathy, reflection
5. **Confusion** - Questions, learning, curiosity
6. **Confidence** - Pride, readiness, trust
7. **Gratitude** - Appreciation, respect, humility

### Movement Cycles

**All characters must maintain consistent:**
- Walk cycle
- Run cycle
- Punch cycle (3 types: Jab, Cross, Hook)
- Kick cycle (3 types: Front, Low, Mid)
- Bow cycle (90° formal, casual)
- Stretch cycle

### Behavioral Consistency

- マサキ: Always upbeat, energetic movements, quick gestures
- メルティ: Gentle, flowing movements, reassuring presence
- アイカ: Calm, deliberate movements, observant posture
- ヒトシ: Crisp, technical movements, confident stance
- ミイチ: Smooth, traditional movements, graceful presence
- アンディ: Slightly futuristic, glowing elements, helpful gestures

## Episode Structure

### Standard Episode Format

**Duration:** 6 min / episode  
**Structure:** 7 × 30-second segments (transitional buffer included)

| Segment | Duration | Content |
|---------|----------|---------|
| Opening | 30s | Scene setup |
| Problem | 30s | Challenge introduced |
| Dialogue | 30s | Teacher explanation |
| Demo | 30s | Teacher shows |
| Practice | 30s | Kids try |
| Celebration | 30s | Success moment |
| Lesson | 30s | Life lesson |

### Series Arc (Planned Episodes 1-10)

1. **EP1:** はじめまして FLATUP GYM （Intro）
2. **EP2:** はじめての礼 （Etiquette）
3. **EP3:** ありがとう （Gratitude）
4. **EP4:** 勇気 （Courage）
5. **EP5:** 仲間 （Friendship）
6. **EP6:** 負けても大丈夫 （Resilience）

## Audio Guidelines

### Voice Direction

- **All voices:** Bright, clear, encouraging
- **No yelling:** Calm communication
- **Sound effects:** Soft punch/kick sounds, gentle equipment sounds
- **Music:** Uplifting, warm, instrumental-focused

### Background Sound

- Children's laughter (gentle)
- Equipment interaction (muffled)
- Breathing (emphasized on effort)
- Footsteps on mat
- Ambient gym hum

## Production Safety Rules

### Content Guidelines

✅ **ALLOWED:**
- Gentle martial arts practice
- Respect and bowing
- Teamwork and encouragement
- Mild effort/focus faces
- Playful competition
- Success celebration

❌ **NOT ALLOWED:**
- Violence or aggression
- Pain expressions
- Injury or blood
- Scary faces
- Bullying or mockery
- Dark or frightening themes

### Character Representation

- All characters are **original designs**
- No resemblance to real FLATUP GYM members (face-wise)
- Diversity in appearance is intentional
- All children shown with agency and happiness
